01 · Where We Started

How we started and what was holding GEM Hospital back

Great doctors and surgical care, but low online visibility and no structured telecalling follow up.

How We Started

Started with a ₹50K Trial, Scaled to Full Content, Camps & Campaigns

We started with a ₹50K to ₹60K/mo trial ad budget (Jan to Mar) when GEM Hospital had just 300 Instagram followers, 150 YouTube subscribers, and relied on static templates without dedicated campaigns for Transplant, Cancer, or regional Special Camps.

Once our doctor videos, special camp funnels, and 2 tier telecalling started bringing real OPD walk ins, we scaled into full Content Creation, Super Specialty Campaigns & Special Medical Camps.

Before D-Medva

Where They Were Lagging

  • 1
    Low Social Reach: Started with just 300 Instagram followers and 150 YouTube subscribers, posting basic stock templates instead of real doctor videos.
  • 2
    Low Special Camp Turnout: Regional medical screening camps lacked targeted digital promotion and structured pre camp patient registration.
  • 3
    Small Trial Ad Budget: Only ₹50K to ₹60K/mo spend (Jan to Mar) with general hospital ads and no separate campaigns for Transplant, Cancer, or Hernia.
  • 4
    Unfiltered Patient Leads: Raw ad enquiries went straight to the hospital desk without phone verification, wasting reception time on non serious enquiries.
After D-Medva

How We Brought Them Up

  • ✓
    Grew Social Reach: Filmed on site doctor podcasts, specialist Q&As, and Reels to reach 11,000 Instagram followers (36.7×) and 16.3K YouTube subscribers (108.7×).
  • ✓
    High Turnout Special Camps: Promoted regional Hernia, Kidney Stone & Free Screening Camps—generating 500 Hernia Camp leads (15.1L reach) and 340 Kidney Stone Camp leads.
  • ✓
    Scaled to 4,500 Leads / Month: Scaled monthly ad spend step by step to ₹4L to ₹5L/mo and ran 30 dedicated Transplant & Cancer ads/mo across India & abroad.
  • ✓
    5 Stage Telecalling Filter: Our D-Medva telecalling team calls and verifies every patient's medical need first before handing over qualified leads to GEM Hospital.

Lead Conversion Flow

5 Stage Telecalling: From Ad Click to OPD Walk In

Every ad and camp enquiry is called, verified, and followed up until the patient visits the doctor.

Stage 01

Lead Comes In

Patient submits enquiry from an ad or camp post.

Stage 02

D-Medva First Call

Our team calls first to verify the patient's medical need.

Stage 03

Hospital Handover

Qualified leads are shared with GEM Hospital's telecaller.

Stage 04

Follow Up Call

Appointment date and doctor slot are confirmed.

Stage 05

OPD Visit

Patient visits GEM Hospital for specialist consultation.

Before vs. Now Paid Campaign Architecture

How We Transformed GEM Hospital's Patient Acquisition Ads

  • ✓
    From Single Lead Forms → 3-Channel Patient Capture: Earlier, ads relied only on basic Instant Forms (51 leads). Now we run parallel Instant Lead Forms (268 leads), Click-to-WhatsApp Ads (192 chats), and Direct Click-to-Call Ads (245 calls) for a single surgical department.
  • ✓
    From City-Only → Split Chennai + All-Over-TN Targeting: Structured separate ad sets for Chennai Metro and All Over Tamil Nadu (Excluding Chennai) so regional surgical & transplant patients are captured alongside local OPD patients.
  • ✓
    Lower Cost Per Patient Enquiry (Down to ₹34.57/Call): Brought down cost per result from ₹84.12–₹101.95 to ₹34.57–₹54.84 per direct phone call and ₹62.59–₹72.56 per WhatsApp conversation.
  • ✓
    10.32L+ Monthly Ad Impressions & 5-Stage Telecalling: Every form lead, WhatsApp message, and inbound call is immediately screened by D-Medva's telecalling desk before booking the specialist OPD slot.
