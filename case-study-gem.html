01 · Where We Started

How we started and what was holding GEM Hospital back

Great doctors and surgical care, but low online visibility and no structured telecalling follow up.

How We Started

Started with a ₹50K Trial, Scaled to 7 Channels

We started with a ₹50K to ₹60K/mo trial ad budget (Jan to Mar) when GEM Hospital had just 300 Instagram followers, 150 YouTube subscribers, and ranked on Page 2 of Google (Pos 11.6).

Once doctor videos and our 2 tier telecalling started bringing real OPD walk ins, we scaled into the full 7 Channel Infinity 360 package.

Before D-Medva

Where They Were Lagging

  • 1
    Low Social Reach: Started with just 300 Instagram followers and 150 YouTube subscribers, posting basic stock templates instead of real doctor videos.
  • 2
    Stuck on Page 2 of Google: Website ranked at Position 11.6 with only 1.8% CTR, missing patients searching for laparoscopy and gastro care in Chennai.
  • 3
    Small Trial Ad Budget: Only ₹50K to ₹60K/mo spend (Jan to Mar) with general hospital ads and no separate campaigns for Transplant, Cancer, or Hernia.
  • 4
    Unfiltered Patient Leads: Raw ad enquiries went straight to the hospital desk without phone verification, wasting reception time on non serious enquiries.
After D-Medva

How We Brought Them Up

  • ✓
    Grew Social Reach: Filmed on site doctor podcasts, specialist Q&As, and Reels to reach 11,000 Instagram followers (36.7×) and 16.3K YouTube subscribers (108.7×).
  • ✓
    Page 1 on Google (#7.7 Rank): Optimized department SEO pages to bring GEM Hospital onto Page 1 with 28.7K monthly clicks, 1.19M impressions, and 2.4% CTR.
  • ✓
    Scaled to 4,500 Leads / Month: Scaled monthly ad spend step by step to ₹4L to ₹5L/mo and ran 30 dedicated Transplant & Cancer ads/mo across India & abroad.
  • ✓
    5 Stage Telecalling Filter: Our D-Medva telecalling team calls and verifies every patient's medical need first before handing over qualified leads to GEM Hospital.

Lead Conversion Flow

5 Stage Telecalling: From Ad Click to OPD Walk In

Every ad and camp enquiry is called, verified, and followed up until the patient visits the doctor.

Stage 01

Lead Comes In

Patient submits enquiry from an ad or camp post.

Stage 02

D-Medva First Call

Our team calls first to verify the patient's medical need.

Stage 03

Hospital Handover

Qualified leads are shared with GEM Hospital's telecaller.

Stage 04

Follow Up Call

Appointment date and doctor slot are confirmed.

Stage 05

OPD Visit

Patient visits GEM Hospital for specialist consultation.
