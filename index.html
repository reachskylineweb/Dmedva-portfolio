PATIENT CONVERSION PIPELINE (SLIDE 13)

5-Stage Telecalling & Consultation Flow

From the first digital enquiry to the doctor’s consultation room, every lead is called, qualified, and followed up systematically.

  1. 1
    Leads Generated

    Patient enquiries arrive from targeted department ads, search, and health camp campaigns.

  2. 2
    Initial Telecalling Qualification

    First prompt outbound call to each lead to understand the requirement and qualify the enquiry.

  3. 3
    Hospital Telecaller Handover

    Qualified patient leads are routed directly to the hospital’s internal telecalling and scheduling desk.

  4. 4
    Structured Follow-up

    Scheduled follow-up calls address scheduling questions and help convert undecided enquiries.

  5. 5
    Doctor Consultation

    The patient confirms their appointment and visits the specialist at the hospital.

How our ad posters build patient trust before the first consultation.

Every Meta Ad and Google Search campaign we run for GEM Hospital, Supreme Speciality Hospital, and Healing Earth Ayurveda is engineered to build immediate medical trust and route enquiries into structured follow-up.

Condition-Specific Visual Clarity

Instead of vague hospital branding, each poster speaks directly to the patient’s exact concern—whether it is blood-group mismatch in organ transplants, untreated hernia pain, non-surgical piles relief, or silent depression—using clear anatomical visuals and zero fear-mongering.

  • Clear symptom-to-solution medical headline
